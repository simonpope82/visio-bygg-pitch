FAQ

Good questions

What do you need from us?

Floor plans, facades and a materials list for the house. PDF works. DWG, IFC or a 3D model from your architect makes it faster.

Is it accurate? AI can make mistakes.

Yes, we build a real 3D model from your drawings – AI only speeds up the work. Every model is checked against the plans before a buyer sees it.

How long does it take?

We agree a turnaround time in the pilot. It depends on the size of the house and the drawings we get.

Do buyers need their own VR headset?

No. We bring headsets to your sales office or show home. Buyers can also watch the 3D film on any phone, tablet or TV.

Who owns the drawings and the film?

Your drawings stay yours. We sign an NDA and only use them for your project. You get full rights to use the film in your own marketing.

What happens when a buyer wants a change?

We show the change in 3D, the buyer approves it, and you get a clear list for your architect and site team.
